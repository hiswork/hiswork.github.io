Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

rclone-backup is used only by its owner, to back up the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

It requests a single Google permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which allows access only to files and folders that rclone-backup itself creates. It cannot see or change any other file in the Drive.

The OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in the rclone configuration file. No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or sent anywhere other than Google Drive. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
